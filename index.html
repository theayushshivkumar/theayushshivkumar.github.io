Hello, and welcome to my website! I am an early-career astrophysicist interested in using observations to uncover the physical processes governing the formation and evolution of stars and planetary systems. I enjoy combining spectroscopy, photometry, and modelling to connect observational signatures with the underlying astrophysics.

I recently graduated with a BS–MS (Integrated Bachelor’s + Master’s) in Physics from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Thiruvananthapuram, with a minor in Data Science. My research focuses on star and planet formation, particularly the physical and chemical evolution of protoplanetary disks. I am especially interested in understanding how accretion, disk chemistry, and gas dynamics influence the environments where planets form, using multi-wavelength observations from facilities such as JWST/MIRI, VLT, CFHT/ESPaDOnS, Keck/NIRSPEC, ALMA, TESS, and other high-resolution spectrographs.

Please see Research for a brief description of my previous projects.

Apart from research, I thoroughly enjoy writing about science, technology, and whatever else scratches my brain on my personal blog! I spend most of my free time listening to music, watching sciency videos, tuning in to podcasts, yapping with friends and family, and watching other people play video games – a strange comfort of mine since I was very little! I enjoy critically analysing the books, films, podcasts, and games I consume, which is a habit that has made its way into how I carry out research as well. I find collaborative and curiosity-driven spaces to be the ideal environment where I can continue learning while contributing to our growing understanding of the universe.

I am currently seeking PhD opportunities in observational astrophysics, with a focus on studying protoplanetary disks, star formation, and circumstellar environments with high-resolution spectroscopy and molecular line modelling.

Feel free to contact me regarding research opportunities, collaborations, or scientific discussions.
